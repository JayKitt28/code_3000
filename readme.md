My views on ethics, relating specfically to technology and AI, believe that it is important to weigh the impacts of decsions before making changes. This idea is talked about in Chapter 5 of Ethical Machines and one thing I took away from this chapter was the idea of "Stating your values by thinking about your ethical nightmares". I think this is a good mindset is to think about the worst case possible and weighing if this decsision to procced outways the ethical nightmare situation that you have though of. Another improtant thing to do is keeping clear communications, both internally and externally. As the book states, its important to share things that people could not reasonabily know or assume so that you are always transparent. There are many other things that go in to being ethical but always thinking about the worst case, preparring for it, and communicating decisions goes a long way to being a ethical person, company, team etc...
